Updating CTBRec Mobile Reviewer v2.11.3 -> v2.12.0
=================================================

Recommended existing-install update
-----------------------------------
1. Stop Mobile Reviewer.
2. Extract the v2.12.0 DROP-IN (or FRAME_CUTS_HOTFIX) over the existing
   CTBRec_Mobile_Reviewer folder and allow the included program files to replace
   older versions.
3. Do NOT delete your existing config, queues, Recu/session state, mosaics, or
   model data.
4. Restart Mobile Reviewer.
5. Fully close/reopen the saved iPhone Home Screen app once (shell cache v2120).

Using Frame Cut
---------------
1. Open a model in Review mode.
2. Tap Frame Cut above the mosaic.
3. Choose Leave for review, Cumshots, or Misc Hot Scenes.
4. Tap the exact sampled frames whose following time intervals you want to keep.
   Consecutive selected frames with the same destination become one output clip.
5. Leave unwanted frames unselected (or use Delete / clear).
6. Tap Queue precise cuts & next.

The phone advances immediately. The durable PC job waits for idle time, then
creates and verifies replacement clips. Only after every required replacement
verifies are the original Review recordings moved to MARKED_FOR_DELETION.

If Frame Cut is disabled for an old Review mosaic, tap Regenerate once. Reviewer
refuses destructive frame-level cutting when an old mosaic does not provide an
exact timestamp map.

Mobile Settings -> Background process priority -> Precise Review frame cuts lets
you change the idle threshold (default 10 minutes).
