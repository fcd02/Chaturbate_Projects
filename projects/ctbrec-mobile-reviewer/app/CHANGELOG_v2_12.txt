CTBRec Mobile Reviewer v2.12.0 — Precise Review Frame Cuts
==========================================================

PURPOSE
-------
v2.12.0 extends the Review sorter from whole-recording decisions to optional
frame-boundary decisions, allowing the user to retain only the useful portions
represented by sampled Review mosaic frames.

PHONE REVIEW WORKFLOW
---------------------
- Review mode now offers two granularities:
  * Whole segments: the established v2.11 behavior.
  * Frame Cut: each mosaic frame is an exact time boundary.
- In Frame Cut mode, choose Leave for review, Cumshots, or Misc Hot Scenes and
  tap individual mosaic frames.
- A selected frame retains the interval beginning at that frame's timestamp and
  ending at the next sampled-frame boundary. Consecutive selected intervals of
  the same destination are merged into one output clip.
- Unselected intervals are discarded; they are not copied into separate waste
  clips.
- Adjacent retained intervals can span CTBRec source-file boundaries. The PC
  concatenates the source timeline and encodes the merged kept range as one
  verified MP4 output.

DURABLE / IDLE PC EXECUTION
---------------------------
- Submit writes a durable review_frame_cuts job and immediately advances the
  phone to the next sortable chunk.
- Precise cut jobs wait until the PC has been idle for the configured threshold
  (default: 10 minutes; adjustable in Mobile Settings).
- Before that idle threshold, whole-library mosaic generation and Non-NSFW
  scanning may continue.
- Once a frame-cut job becomes idle-eligible it outranks both whole-library
  mosaic generation and Non-NSFW scanning.
- Interactive/current-phone mosaic work still outranks frame cutting. If the PC
  becomes active or the phone requests mosaic work while ffmpeg is cutting, the
  temporary output is stopped/removed and the durable job resumes later without
  consuming a failure retry.

DATA SAFETY
-----------
- Review mosaics now persist exact per-frame chunk timestamp, local source
  timestamp, and interval-end timestamp in the embedded sidecar layout.
- Older embedded Review layouts are accepted only when their exact sampling
  timeline can be reconstructed safely. Legacy hit maps without exact timing
  require one mosaic regeneration before Frame Cut can be enabled.
- Output clips are written to job-specific temporary paths, verified for file
  size and duration, atomically committed, and reverified.
- Every replacement clip must verify before ANY original Review recording is
  permitted to leave its source folder.
- After all outputs verify, every original source recording from that Review
  chunk is moved through the existing reversible MARKED_FOR_DELETION path.
- Output names contain the durable job token so retries/restarts can safely
  recognize their own outputs without overwriting unrelated files.
- Changed source size/mtime after the decision is a safety block; the job will
  not silently cut different bytes than the mosaic represented.
- Back/Undo stops an in-progress frame cut, restores any moved originals, and
  removes only the replacement clips uniquely created by that durable job.

OUTPUT DESTINATIONS
-------------------
- Leave for review -> <model>\Review
- Cumshots         -> <model>\Cumshots
- Misc Hot Scenes  -> <model>\Misc Hot Scenes
- Delete / unselected -> no replacement output; original source ultimately goes
  to the drive's MARKED_FOR_DELETION path after kept outputs verify.

COMPATIBILITY
-------------
- Whole-segment Review sorting is unchanged and remains available beside Frame
  Cut mode.
- Existing mobile config/state/queue/Recu/live-bridge data is not required to be
  replaced. The new frame-cut idle setting defaults to 10 minutes when absent.
- PWA shell cache namespace is v2120. Fully close/reopen the iPhone Home Screen
  app once after installing.
